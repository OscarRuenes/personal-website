About me

Hey, I'm Oscar! I received my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from The University of Texas at Dallas in the Erik Jonsson School of Engineering and Computer Science as well as a certificate in Data Science. Academically, I am interested in researching Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning and I've applied to Master's programs at The University of Texas at Austin. My overarching goal for my professional career is to pursue software development to help build reliable, robust, and intelligent software systems that others can depend on.

I made this website to compile some previous projects and to motivate myself to create more. Feel free to check out some of my projects in the navigation bar at the top of the webpage!

I am excited to start my journey of entering the industry as well as honing in my craft in a masters program, and I am still deciding between looking into Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, or Data Science. In my free time, I enjoy spending time with my family, working out, playing drumset, and playing video games with my friends and girlfriend.
